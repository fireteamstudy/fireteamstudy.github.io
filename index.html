NTN Math Speed Drills

Train your mental math for the NTN
Two drill modes built around the patterns that came up on practice test 2: hose-section conversions (multiplying and dividing by 50) and square-footage fraction estimates.
How it works
Pick a mode at the top. Each question has a timer. Answer before it runs out, or it counts as wrong. Hit Enter or Space to advance to the next question after answering.
Hose Sections mode
A section of fire hose is 50 feet. Convert between feet and sections using mental shortcuts.
Feet → Sections
Given a length in feet, how many 50-ft sections do you need? Always rounds up. Example: 237 ft → 5 sections (since 4 would only give 200 ft).
Trick: divide by 50 = double, then drop two zeros. 237 → round up to 250 → double to 500 → drop zeros = 5.
Sections → Feet
Given a number of sections, how many feet total? Pure ×50. Example: 27 sections → 1,350 ft.
Trick: halve the sections, add two zeros. 27 → 13.5 → 1,350.
Mixed
Random mix of both directions. Use this once each direction feels comfortable on its own.
Digit hints: the underscores under the input show how many digits the answer has. Three underscores means a 3-digit answer, etc. Use it to sanity-check your mental math before you submit.
Fractions mode
Given a total square footage and a portion of it, pick the closest fraction from five options:
A 1/10
B 1/4
C 1/2
D 3/4
E 9/10
Example: a 1,200 sq ft house, 700 sq ft is closest to 1/2 (which would be 600). 1/4 would be 300 and 3/4 would be 900, so 700 sits closest to half.
Trick: don't compute portion ÷ total. Instead, compute each fraction of the total and see which is closest. For 1,200: 1/10 = 120, 1/4 = 300, 1/2 = 600, 3/4 = 900, 9/10 = 1,080.
Keyboard shortcuts: press A–E or 1–5 to answer instantly.
Timer and stats
The bar at the top fills from green to red as your time runs out. Default is 4 seconds — adjust in settings, or turn the timer off entirely if you want to focus on accuracy first.
Stats track correct, wrong, current streak, and average response time. Watch the average drop as you drill — that's the real progress signal.
Suggested workout
Five minutes a day:
  1. 2 min on Sections → Feet at 4s
  2. 2 min on Feet → Sections at 4s
  3. 1 min on Fractions at 4s
Once your average is under 2 seconds on each, drop the timer to 3s.
